# Visual Phonema — long-form audiovisual study

User direction (2026-11-27): develop an original Spoonexx audiovisual work of 8–15 minutes, alongside the broader visual language. The work should guide technical experiments, not become a compilation of demos.

## Working hypothesis (not an approved score)
Reference duration: 10 minutes. Six movements: genesis, organization, friction, suspension, catharsis, residue. Durations, sound, words, palette and narrative remain open. Recurrent motifs should transform, accumulate consequences and return in altered form. Use silence, negative space and camera independently of sound energy. Avoid constant novelty, constant intensity and simplistic beat/frequency mapping.

## Study 012.1
A deterministic 15-second silent visual study: assembly, apparent extrusion, an independent moving object, rupture and residue. Native Canvas 2D; faux depth, not true 3D. A provisional palette and PHONEMA text are placeholders. Play/pause/scrub; no audio or export yet. This is an isolated study, not a scene accepted for the final work.

## Validation
Observe on iPad/iPhone and desktop; assess typography, materiality, depth, spatial pacing, motion coherence and whether the visual events have expressive purpose. Only after observation decide what to retain and test in a 45–90 second sound-linked sequence. Preserve all historical experiments. No changes to engine, runtime or published index in this branch.

## Later work
Choose musical source or compose sound with the image; define a time-coded cue score and event interface; test transitions and motif memory between passages; prototype frame-accurate rendering/export. Final duration is subject to artistic validation.
